If you've ever had spender's regret after purchasing a
cute outfit that you only wear twice a year, this is the program to help.
It can be quite hard to decide what clothes to get when you go to Zara
or H&M and see dozens of cute tops and charming salesperson and suddenly you
walk out with a top that you'll wear once and never again.

When shopping for clothes, it can also be hard to decide as you scroll through endless
categories, fits, colors, and more.

Especially when you want to treat yourself, it can be hard to know what's worth
splurging and what's a waste of money.

Before trying out the program, catalogue your wardrobe using our template
here: (https://1drv.ms/x/c/c6adbb0a027c3b55/IQCqP2Yc4G5lTbDK-S2wRgGmAVEL6O7zLco9JekSqhNCITA?e=YH8hcI)
To ensure your file is read correctly: Place your completed wardrobe Excel file in the project folder before running the program.

This program aims to eliminate this decision fatigue and help you be more finanically
effective in your purchases by learning your lifestyle habits, goals, and
calculating the cost per wear based on that information to give you the best
recommendations.

Happy shopping!
